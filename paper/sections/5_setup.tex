\section{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Evaluation sets.}
\label{sec:setup:data}
Training corpora are in \Cref{tab:corpora}. We evaluate on SNLI \citep{bowman2015snli}, MNLI
\citep{williams2018mnli}, ANLI \citep{nie2020anli}, BoolQ \citep{clark2019boolq}, CLINC-150
\citep{larson2019clinc}, TREC-fine, HWU64, 20 Newsgroups and Banking77 \citep{casanueva2020banking77};
a fixed 1{,}200-item MMLU-Pro probe \citep{wang2024mmlupro} and TruthfulQA-MC1 \citep{lin2022truthfulqa};
held-out generator splits and ChaosNLI \citep{nie2020chaosnli}; 605 held-out long states; and the
evaluation-only external sets PagerDuty, jevlogs, Mind2Web, tree-choice, typed-decisions and JevBench.
\label{sec:setup:heldout}
Each held-out split excludes a whole family, domain, rubric style, grammar or parameter range from
training; none is a random validation split (\Cref{tab:generalization}). Set sizes and floors, metric
definitions and the latency protocol are in \Cref{app:protocol}.

\paragraph{\texorpdfstring{$\dq$}{Delta-q}: question dependence separated from candidate priors.}
\label{sec:setup:deltaq}

\begin{definition}[Question-dependence gap]
\label{def:deltaq}
$\dq = \mathrm{Acc}\big(f(\state, \query, \cands)\big) - \mathrm{Acc}\big(f(\state, \tilde{\query},
\cands)\big)$, where $\tilde{\query}$ replaces the real question with the state and candidate set held
fixed: an empty placeholder in the \emph{choices-only} variant, a real unrelated question from the same
corpus in the \emph{shuffled-question} variant $\dqsh$, primary throughout.
\end{definition}

$\dq$ extends the choices-only control of \citet{balepur2024artifacts} to a shuffled real question, which
is stricter: a placeholder can leak candidate priors through the rendering (on one candidate-blind model
choices-only reads $+.017$ where $\dqsh$ reads $.000$). High accuracy with $\dqsh \approx 0$ means that
candidate-set priors explain the score. The reference is the listwise teacher, a fine-tuned decoder
reading the options in context, at $\dqsh = .102$ on the MMLU-Pro probe. $|\dqsh| < .02$ (about 1.4
standard errors at $n{=}1{,}200$, $p \approx .15$) counts as zero.

\paragraph{JevBench protocol.}
\label{sec:setup:jevbench}
JevBench (\texttt{fstandhartinger/jevbench} v1.2.1) is a third-party benchmark of yes/no, choice
($\numcands{=}2$--6) and ordinal decisions in three tiers: \emph{standard} (72 items), \emph{easy} (48)
and \emph{hard} (111). Every JevBench number here is on the 231 public ids and unranked; no training
corpus contains a public id. The 72 standard items are 36 states in two paraphrases, so intervals are
cluster bootstraps over the paraphrase group (20{,}000 resamples): $\pm6$--13 points on standard, $\pm9$
on hard. Comparisons between our models use paired per-item bootstraps (\Cref{app:protocol,app:leaderboard}).
