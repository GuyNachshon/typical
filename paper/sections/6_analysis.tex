\section{Where the decision lives}
\label{sec:analysis}

Unless stated otherwise, models here are 1.7B, trained for 12k steps on the evidence and knowledge
mixture, and $\dqsh$ is measured on the MMLU-Pro probe against the teacher's .102.

\subsection{The contextual-candidate readout}
\label{sec:analysis:readout}

Scoring the decision state against each candidate's contextual state keeps the letter-logit
readout's question-conditioned signal within seed noise, with a quarter of its pairwise log-odds shift
and 2--7 more points on natural language inference. \Cref{tab:n1n3} compares the readouts of
\Cref{sec:method:readout} on one pass: letter logits (N1), $\hD$ against an independent embedding of each
option's text (N2), and $\hD$ against each option's contextual state $\hctx{j}$ (N3). All arms render the
candidates in the suffix, train on identical data with per-step option shuffling, and read all 28 layers.
N2 and N3 differ only in what $\hD$ is scored against.

The candidate's contextual state carries the decision. N2 loses 10--12 points of among-$\numcands$
accuracy against N3 and 13 against N1, half of the question-conditioned signal ($\dqsh$ .058 vs.\ .106
for N3 across seeds), and collapses on Banking77 at $\numcands{=}77$ (.012). The pattern fits a decision
state that encodes which rendered slot holds the answer: the letter embedding and the contextual state
both carry slot identity, and an independently embedded label carries only its meaning.

N3 matches N1 on question dependence and is far less sensitive to the candidate set. Across seeds N1
averages $\dqsh = .109$ and N3 .106, within the .01--.02 seed spread of both; among-$\numcands$ accuracy
is 1--2 points lower for N3. Adding three unrelated options shifts N3's pairwise log-odds by .09--.13
against .44--.46 for N1, and reordering moves its probabilities by .10--.11 against .165. On evidence
tasks N3 leads in both seeds by 2 points on SNLI, 5.5--7 on MNLI and 4--5 on ANLI, and trails by 2--3 on
BoolQ. Option shuffling is shared, so the order-sensitivity difference comes from the readout together
with its null form. N3 is the readout Typical deploys; exact order invariance is the Bernoulli head's
contract (\Cref{sec:results:typed}).

\subsection{Candidate-blind decision states}
\label{sec:analysis:deltaq}

In every configuration we tested, question-conditioned capability appeared only when the
candidates were processed inside the forward pass. A candidate-blind variant computes a decision state
$Z(\state, \query)$ before the candidates are seen, scores each candidate against it with a learned head,
and is distilled from the listwise teacher. Six such variants, spanning three head factorizations, two
distillation objectives and two tap depths, all have $\dqsh$ within .03 of zero, while N1, N3 and the
teacher sit at .09--.12 and even N2 keeps .058 (\Cref{fig:deltaq}; per-variant numbers in
\Cref{tab:blind}).

No lever we varied moves $\dqsh$, including cross-attention over all candidate tokens, multi-set
log-odds supervision and full-depth reading (\Cref{app:extra-figures}). We cannot exclude optimization:
the zero-initialized set-conditioning cross-attention stayed inert through 4k steps of direct
supervision. Accuracy hides the gap because candidate-blind states learn priors and calibration. Their accuracy of
.12--.17 sits above the .10 chance rate and comes from which option strings tend to be correct together,
and ECE on the knowledge probe falls from .39 for an earlier baseline to .04. The question-dependent
component lives in candidate-contextual computation, so Typical renders the candidates inside the
backbone's forward pass. Any architecture that scores candidates against a precomputed representation
should report $\dq$ alongside accuracy.

\subsection{Mid-depth readout}
\label{sec:analysis:depth}

Reading at 71\% of depth gives one model that carries both evidence reasoning and
question-conditioned knowledge. The first full-scale model in this project read the last layer and
scored 58.6 on SNLI, the hypothesis-only baseline (65.3 frozen and 68.2 with LoRA at layer 20). With
head, data and steps fixed, reading N3 at layer 20 instead of 28 raises SNLI/MNLI/ANLI/BoolQ from
.863/.752/.431/.737 to .906/.865/.519/.834, CLINC-150 from .740 to .862, and lowers validation NLL from
.430 to .341, while $\dqsh$ stays at .118 against .117. Evidence accuracy lands within 1--4 points of a
candidate-blind energy-scoring baseline on every evidence set (ANLI trails by 3.6) and 8 points above it
on CLINC-150, so one model serves the evidence and knowledge regimes that had needed separate models.
For a candidate-blind state the same change moves nothing. Decision features below the final,
next-token-specialized layers agree with work on layer pruning and probing
\citep{gromov2024deeperlayers,men2024shortgpt,tenney2019pipeline,belrose2023tunedlens}. Every reported
checkpoint uses the 71\% tap: layer 20 of 28, 26 of 36 and 28 of 40.

\subsection{Abstention as a gate}
\label{sec:analysis:null}

Abstention belongs in the gate alone. A rendered ``none of the above'' line competes for softmax mass
with the real candidates, so its share depends on how many there are. A matched pair tests this: both
arms are N3 at layer 20 with the factored gate of \Cref{eq:factored-null}, and the treatment removes the
rendered $\nullsym$ line. With the line, abstention has degenerate endpoints: with the gold label absent,
the model almost never abstains on CLINC-150 at $\numcands{=}5$ ($P(\nullsym \mid \text{absent}) = .02$)
and always abstains at $\numcands{=}150$ (1.00) and on Banking77 at $\numcands{=}77$. Removing the line
removes both endpoints (null AUROC by $\numcands$ in \Cref{app:extra-figures}). MMLU-Pro false abstention
falls from .692 to .003 (far outside its seed-to-seed variation of .05--.10) and accuracy rises from .133
to .353, Banking77 with all 77 labels rises from .063 to .579, and CLINC out-of-scope accuracy from .639
to .798, with $\dqsh$ unchanged (.118 vs.\ .122) and evidence tasks level (\Cref{tab:app-null-fix}). The
price is 2--7 points on intent and topic label spaces and a rise in reorder $\Delta p$. This configuration, contextual readout at 71\% depth with a gate and no rendered
$\nullsym$ line, is the architecture of every checkpoint in \Cref{sec:results}; null augmentation teaches
the gate when to fire (\Cref{sec:results:mixture}).
