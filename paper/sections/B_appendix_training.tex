\subsection{Cached-state equivalence}
\label{app:cache-equivalence}

An equivalence test on the real model checks that the cached-state pass of \Cref{sec:method:pass} computes
the same decision as a forward pass on $\mathrm{concat}(\state, \query, \cands)$. Cached-state decisions
match the full-concatenation path to within $10^{-3}$ across suffix chunk sizes 1, 2, 3, 5 and 64, are
consistent under permutation of the queries in a batch, and repeat exactly.

\subsection{Long-state corpus statistics}
\label{app:long-state-stats}

States in \texttt{data\_wf\_long} have p10/p50/p90 lengths of 1{,}190, 1{,}845 and 2{,}490 tokens. In the
original render, with the case facts after the policy body, right-truncation at a 1{,}024-token window
removed the facts from 98.8\% of rows, and at 256 tokens from all of them. The facts-first render places
the case inside the first 8\% of the text, and \texttt{--drop\_truncated} removes any row longer than the
training window.
