\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Decisions in hidden states.} Hidden states carry linearly decodable structure
\citep{alain2017probes,hewitt2019controltasks,belinkov2022probing} that varies with depth
\citep{tenney2019pipeline,belrose2023tunedlens} and anticipates tokens not yet emitted
\citep{pal2023futurelens,wu2024planahead}, and circuit analysis localizes where a chosen option binds to
its symbol \citep{lieberum2023circuit}. \citet{wong2026models} decode the winning option in content space
before the symbol is bound, and \citet{bhatt2026representation} trace this across languages. Typical
deploys that pre-symbol decision as the interface, read at mid-depth, where upper layers are partly
redundant and early exit saves compute \citep{gromov2024deeperlayers,men2024shortgpt,schwartz2020righttool}.

\paragraph{Answer symbols and runtime labels.} Answer symbols carry order and symbol biases
\citep{zheng2024selectionbias,pezeshkpour2024optionorder,yang2025option,xue2024symbolbinding}, first-token
probabilities disagree with generated text \citep{wang2024firsttoken}, and constrained decoding
\citep{geng2023grammarconstrained,willard2023guided} can cost accuracy \citep{tam2024speakfreely}. $\dq$
(\Cref{def:deltaq}) extends the choices-only control of \citet{balepur2024artifacts} to a shuffled real
question. Runtime labels have been scored by joint embeddings, entailment, label-conditioned encoders and
schemas \citep{pappas2019gile,yin2019zeroshot,halder2020tars,zaratiana2023gliner,zaratiana2025gliner2},
by cross-encoders \citep{nogueira2019bertrerank}, dual encoders \citep{reimers2019sbert}, late interaction
and decomposition
\citep{humeau2020polyencoders,khattab2020colbert,cao2020deformer,macavaney2020prettr,dejong2023lumen} and
distilled students \citep{lu2022erniesearch}, while decoder classification heads fix labels as indices
\citep{li2023labelsupervised}. Typical renders all candidates in one causal suffix, so each carries its
slot and its set, and $\dq$ measures what candidate-independent scoring gives up (\Cref{sec:analysis:deltaq}).

\paragraph{Computation per decision.} Prefix sharing is established systems work
\citep{juravsky2024hydragen,zheng2023sglang,kwon2023pagedattention,gim2023promptcache,cheng2023batch,pope2023scaling,dao2022flashattention},
and speculative decoding targets sequential decode overhead
\citep{leviathan2023speculative,chen2023speculativesampling,cai2024medusa}; Typical removes the decode
loop. Chain-of-thought supplies serial computation a fixed-depth pass lacks
\citep{wei2022cot,li2024cotserial,merrill2024expressive,merrill2023parallelism,feng2023chain}, mainly
helping math and symbolic tasks \citep{sprague2025cot}, as do pause tokens
\citep{goyal2024pause,pfau2024filler} and latent reasoning
\citep{deng2023implicitcot,deng2024internalize,hao2024coconut} (\Cref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{Output contracts and training.} Score uses a soft ordinal target
\citep{mccullagh1980ordinal,frank2001ordinal,niu2016ordinalcnn,cao2020coral,shi2023corn,diaz2019softordinal};
the reject option \citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective,geifman2019selectivenet}, scored by
confidence or energy \citep{hendrycks2017msp,liu2020energyood} and benchmarked on out-of-scope intents
\citep{larson2019clinc,casanueva2020banking77}, becomes a gate outside the softmax
(\Cref{sec:method:null}). Temperature scaling repairs much miscalibration \citep{guo2017calibration},
binned ECE is biased \citep{naeini2015calibration,kumar2019verifiedcalibration}, and we evaluate with
proper scoring rules \citep{gneiting2007scoringrules,brier1950verification,epstein1969rps,murphy1970rps}. Label smoothing
\citep{szegedy2016labelsmoothing,mueller2019labelsmoothing}, distillation
\citep{hinton2015distillation,kim2016seqkd,yuan2020teacherfree,zhang2020selfdistillation} and context
distillation \citep{askell2021assistant,snell2022contextdistillation} shape probabilities, and fine-tuning
distorts calibration \citep{desai2020calibration,wang2025cogcalib} and features
\citep{kumar2022finetunedistort}, rivals in-context learning \citep{mosbach2023fewshot}, and under LoRA
\citep{hu2022lora} learns and forgets less \citep{biderman2024loralearnsless} yet forgets under continual training
\citep{luo2025forgetting}.
Compositional benchmarks \citep{lake2018scan,keysers2020cfq,kim2020cogs,hupkes2020compositionality}
motivate our held-out composition level (\Cref{sec:train:rubric}); long-context sensitivity to evidence
placement \citep{liu2024lostmiddle,levy2024moretokens,shi2023distracted,hsieh2024ruler} and silent pipeline
defects \citep{sambasivan2021datacascades} motivate our render-crossed design (\Cref{sec:results:long}).
JevBench targets closed-weight ``System-One'' decision models such as TypeSafe's Jev; among its open
entries only GLiNER2 \citep{zaratiana2025gliner2} and one ModernBERT-based entry
\citep{warner2024modernbert} have method papers.
