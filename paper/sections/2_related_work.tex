\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Reading decisions from hidden states.} Hidden states carry linearly decodable structure
\citep{alain2017probes,hewitt2019controltasks,belinkov2022probing}, different depths carry different
abstractions \citep{tenney2019pipeline,belrose2023tunedlens}, states anticipate tokens not yet emitted
\citep{pal2023futurelens,wu2024planahead}, and circuit analysis localizes the step that binds a chosen
option to its symbol \citep{lieberum2023circuit}. The closest prior work is \citet{wong2026models}: in
ordinary multiple-choice prompting the winning option is decodable in content space at the option
boundary, before the answer symbol is bound, and \citet{bhatt2026representation} trace the same
emergence across languages. Typical makes that pre-symbol decision the deployed interface and measures
what the interface needs: candidates inside the forward pass (\Cref{sec:analysis:deltaq}), a readout
over each candidate's contextual state (\Cref{sec:analysis:readout}), and a mid-depth tap, consistent
with evidence that upper layers are partly redundant and that early exit is a viable efficiency lever
\citep{gromov2024deeperlayers,men2024shortgpt,schwartz2020righttool}.

\paragraph{The answer-symbol interface.} Answer symbols bring option-order and symbol-identity biases
\citep{zheng2024selectionbias,pezeshkpour2024optionorder,yang2025option}, motivate binding fixes
\citep{xue2024symbolbinding}, and yield first-token probabilities that disagree with the generated text
\citep{wang2024firsttoken}. Constrained decoding produces structured outputs
\citep{geng2023grammarconstrained,willard2023guided} and can cost accuracy \citep{tam2024speakfreely}.
The choices-only control of \citet{balepur2024artifacts} measures how much multiple-choice accuracy
needs no question. Typical removes the symbol from the readout, and $\dq$ (\Cref{def:deltaq}) extends the
choices-only control to a shuffled real question.

\paragraph{Runtime-defined label spaces.} Unseen labels have been scored through joint input--label
embeddings \citep{pappas2019gile}, entailment reformulation \citep{yin2019zeroshot}, label-conditioned
encoders \citep{halder2020tars}, and runtime schemas encoded jointly with the text
\citep{zaratiana2023gliner,zaratiana2025gliner2}. Cost follows the cross- versus dual-encoder trade-off:
one forward pass per candidate \citep{nogueira2019bertrerank}, a candidate-independent dot product
\citep{reimers2019sbert}, or late interaction and decomposition in between
\citep{humeau2020polyencoders,khattab2020colbert,cao2020deformer,macavaney2020prettr,dejong2023lumen},
often with a cross-encoder compiled into a cheaper student \citep{lu2022erniesearch}. Decoder models with
a classification head fix the labels as indices \citep{li2023labelsupervised}. Typical renders all
candidates in one causal suffix over a cached state, so each candidate's representation carries its slot
and its set, and one state prefix serves many decisions. $\dq$ measures what candidate-independent
factorizations give up (\Cref{sec:analysis:deltaq}); such a scorer stays useful as a shortlisting front
end for large sets (\Cref{sec:results:cost}).

\paragraph{Prefix sharing and the decode loop.} Prefix sharing across suffixes is established systems
work \citep{juravsky2024hydragen,zheng2023sglang,kwon2023pagedattention,gim2023promptcache,cheng2023batch,
pope2023scaling,dao2022flashattention}, and speculative decoding and Medusa exist because sequential
decode steps are overhead-bound \citep{leviathan2023speculative,chen2023speculativesampling,cai2024medusa}.
Typical adds no kernel; it deletes the decode loop and reports the resulting cost from 1.7B to 14B
(\Cref{sec:results:cost}).

\paragraph{Ordinal targets, abstention and probability quality.} Score uses the soft-target member of
the ordinal-regression family
\citep{mccullagh1980ordinal,frank2001ordinal,niu2016ordinalcnn,cao2020coral,shi2023corn,diaz2019softordinal}.
The reject option is classical \citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective,geifman2019selectivenet},
scored by confidence or energy \citep{hendrycks2017msp,liu2020energyood} and benchmarked by out-of-scope
intent detection \citep{larson2019clinc,casanueva2020banking77}; Typical makes abstention a gate outside
the candidate softmax with exact algebraic properties (\Cref{sec:method:null}). Temperature scaling
repairs much in-distribution miscalibration \citep{guo2017calibration}, binned ECE is biased
\citep{naeini2015calibration,kumar2019verifiedcalibration}, and proper scoring rules, including the
ordinal ranked probability score, are the primary target
\citep{gneiting2007scoringrules,brier1950verification,epstein1969rps,murphy1970rps}. Label smoothing
\citep{szegedy2016labelsmoothing,mueller2019labelsmoothing} and distillation
\citep{hinton2015distillation,kim2016seqkd,yuan2020teacherfree,zhang2020selfdistillation} improve
probability quality, context distillation compiles a prompt into weights
\citep{askell2021assistant,snell2022contextdistillation}, and fine-tuning can distort calibration
\citep{desai2020calibration,wang2025cogcalib}. Typical shapes probability quality at training time with
soft and ordinal targets, a Brier term and likelihood-based selection (\Cref{sec:train:selection}); its
distillation teacher is the same backbone under a three-shot rendering.

\paragraph{Fine-tuning and composition.} Fine-tuning can distort pretrained features
\citep{kumar2022finetunedistort}, is close to in-context learning under fair comparison
\citep{mosbach2023fewshot}, and with LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} learns and forgets less
\citep{biderman2024loralearnsless} while still forgetting under continual training
\citep{luo2025forgetting}. Compositional benchmarks show where learned grammars stop extending
\citep{lake2018scan,keysers2020cfq,kim2020cogs,hupkes2020compositionality}. Typical's counterfactual
rubric groups make the rubric the only way to fit the corpus, and its level-7 split is a held-out
composition probe (\Cref{sec:train:rubric}).

\paragraph{Long context and data pipelines.} Evidence position, length and distractors change long-context
answers \citep{liu2024lostmiddle,levy2024moretokens,shi2023distracted,hsieh2024ruler}, and pipeline
defects propagate silently into models \citep{sambasivan2021datacascades}. Typical treats evidence
placement as a training factor crossed with evaluation render (\Cref{sec:train:long,sec:results:long}).

\paragraph{Single-pass computation.} Chain-of-thought adds serial computation that a constant-depth
transformer lacks in one pass \citep{wei2022cot,li2024cotserial,merrill2024expressive,
merrill2023parallelism,feng2023chain} and helps mainly on math and symbolic tasks
\citep{sprague2025cot}; pause and filler tokens \citep{goyal2024pause,pfau2024filler} and implicit or
latent reasoning \citep{deng2023implicitcot,deng2024internalize,hao2024coconut} supply that computation
without verbalized steps. Typical is a single fixed-depth pass and a natural terminal layer for such
methods (\Cref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{Typed decision benchmarks.} JevBench targets the interface of closed-weight commercial
``System-One'' decision models such as TypeSafe's Jev, documented only by vendor pages and a third-party
black-box probe. Its open leaderboard entries publish weights and scores, and only GLiNER2
\citep{zaratiana2025gliner2} and the ModernBERT backbone of one entry \citep{warner2024modernbert} have
method papers. Typical addresses the same interface with an open architecture, matched ablations and
released weights.
