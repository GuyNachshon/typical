\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Decisions in hidden states.} Probes recover linearly decodable structure from hidden states
\citep{alain2017probes}, and that structure changes with depth \citep{tenney2019pipeline,belrose2023tunedlens}.
Circuit analysis localizes where a multiple-choice model binds its chosen option to a symbol
\citep{lieberum2023circuit}, and \citet{wong2026models} decode the winning option in content space
before that binding happens, a result \citet{bhatt2026representation} trace across languages. Typical
deploys the pre-symbol decision as the interface and reads it at mid-depth, where upper layers are
partly redundant \citep{gromov2024deeperlayers,men2024shortgpt}.

\paragraph{Answer symbols and runtime labels.} Letter answers carry order and symbol biases
\citep{zheng2024selectionbias,pezeshkpour2024optionorder}, first-token probabilities disagree with the
text a model generates \citep{wang2024firsttoken}, and format constraints can cost accuracy
\citep{tam2024speakfreely}. $\dq$ (\Cref{def:deltaq}) extends the choices-only control of
\citet{balepur2024artifacts} to a shuffled real question. Runtime labels have been scored through
entailment \citep{yin2019zeroshot}, joint schema encoders \citep{zaratiana2025gliner2}, cross-encoders
\citep{nogueira2019bertrerank}, dual encoders \citep{reimers2019sbert} and late interaction
\citep{humeau2020polyencoders,khattab2020colbert}, and decoder classification heads fix labels as
indices \citep{li2023labelsupervised}. Typical renders all candidates in one causal suffix, so each
candidate's state carries its slot and its set, and $\dq$ measures what candidate-independent scoring
gives up (\Cref{sec:analysis:deltaq}).

\paragraph{Computation per decision.} Prefix caching and sharing are established serving techniques
\citep{kwon2023pagedattention,zheng2023sglang,gim2023promptcache}, and speculative decoding shortens the
decode loop \citep{leviathan2023speculative}; Typical has no decode loop. Chain-of-thought supplies the
serial computation that a fixed-depth pass lacks \citep{wei2022cot,merrill2023parallelism,feng2023chain,
merrill2024expressive}, as do pause tokens \citep{goyal2024pause} and latent reasoning
\citep{hao2024coconut}; \Cref{sec:discussion} connects this to the decisions Typical finds hardest.

\paragraph{Output contracts and training.} Score's soft ordinal target follows ordinal regression
\citep{frank2001ordinal,cao2020coral}. The abstention gate descends from the reject option
\citep{chow1970reject,geifman2017selective} and confidence-based out-of-scope detection
\citep{hendrycks2017msp,larson2019clinc}. We evaluate probabilities with proper scoring rules
\citep{gneiting2007scoringrules} alongside temperature-scaled calibration \citep{guo2017calibration},
and train with LoRA \citep{hu2022lora} and distillation \citep{hinton2015distillation}. Compositional
generalization benchmarks \citep{lake2018scan,keysers2020cfq} motivate the held-out composition level of
DecisionMix (\Cref{sec:train:rubric}), and work on evidence position in long contexts
\citep{liu2024lostmiddle} and silent data-pipeline defects \citep{sambasivan2021datacascades} motivates
the render-crossed design (\Cref{sec:results:long}). Among JevBench's open entries, only GLiNER2
\citep{zaratiana2025gliner2} and a ModernBERT-based model \citep{warner2024modernbert} have method
papers.
